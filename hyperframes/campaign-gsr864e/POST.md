## Description

```
India's draft laboratory animal rules, G.S.R. 864(E), would roll back protections in force since 1998.

• Rules 5(1)–(3): stray, feral and even protected wild animals could be taken for experiments, on the word of an undefined "competent authority".
• Rule 21(2): reopens open-market sourcing of livestock, fish and birds.
• Rule 11: severe experiments on mice, rats and rabbits have no stated approver.
• The 1998 duty to give anaesthesia (Rule 9(d)) is dropped.

Some parts of the draft are better. But these losses are major. We ask for sixteen clause-level fixes, or a full redraft.
```
