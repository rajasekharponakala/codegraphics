## Description

```
Masala Lab Atlas is a free, interactive website for the science of Indian cooking: a fan-made study companion to Krish Ashok's book Masala Lab.

Try it: https://rajasekharponakala.github.io/scicook/

In this Short:
• Heat: drag from 0 to 480 °C and watch water molecules speed up.
• Pressure: why dal takes forever in Shimla (water boils at about 92 °C there), and how a pressure cooker fixes it.
• Spices: 23 spices linked by the flavour molecules they share.
• Onions: brown one stage by stage, from raw to jammy.
• Acid: most balanced dishes sit between pH 4.3 and 4.9.
• Umami: the glutamate + nucleotide synergy model.
• Microwave: why a flat papad gets cold stripes, and why rolling it in a glass works.
• Generators for gravies, breads and brines, and a 6-track biryani planner.

Unofficial and fan-made. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Krish Ashok or the publisher. Page references on the site point to the book: please buy it.
All footage is a screen recording of the site. Voice: AI text-to-speech (Indian English). Music: original.

#Shorts #IndianCooking #FoodScience #Cooking #MasalaLab
```
