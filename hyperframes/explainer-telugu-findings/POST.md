## Description

```
Telugudanam (తెలుగుదనం), an online museum of the Telugu language, explores it in five modules. Here is what each one finds:

SCRIPT
• Born of Ashoka's Brahmi, Telugu had its own script by the 11th century.
• Its letters are round because a straight stroke would split a palm leaf.
• Its ancestor, the Pallava script, seeded Khmer, Javanese, Thai and Lao.

LANGUAGE
• Telugu is Dravidian, not a daughter of Sanskrit; its closest relative is Tulu.
• One word can carry a whole sentence: చేయించలేదు, "did not cause to do".
• English borrowed from Telugu: catamaran, bandicoot.

LITERATURE
• It began in the mouth: Burra Katha, Oggu Katha, Vemana.
• Three poets across three centuries wrote one Telugu Mahabharata.
• Kanyasulkam (1892) and Sri Sri (1950) brought the street into literature.

DIASPORA
• A thousand years of migration, from Southeast Asia to the Gulf and America.
• About 1,500 Telugus in the US in 1970; over 1.2 million by 2024.
• The test is the second generation.

DIGITAL
• From broken fonts to Telugu Wikipedia (2003) and Unicode (2004).
• Tenglish: Telugu typed in the Latin alphabet.
• Open source, including Swecha's Gonthuka speech model.
```
