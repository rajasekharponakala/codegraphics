## Description

```
From 2011 to 2023, the share of Indians below $4.20 a day fell from 57.7% to 16.1%: an estimated 496 million people, according to the World Bank.

• Extreme poverty ($3.00/day) fell from 27.1% to 2.6%.
• Main drivers, in the brief's words: strong consumption growth, rising employment (especially among women) and social transfers, including food subsidies.
• Still poor (at $4.20/day, 2023): rural 19.3% vs urban 8.6%; children 21.8%.
• About 57% of India's poor live in a belt from Uttar Pradesh to West Bengal.
• The top 10% of workers earn about 13× the bottom 10%. Youth unemployment rose from 14.6% to 15.9%.
• Projected: 12.1% by 2028.

Caveats from the brief: part of the 2022–24 gain likely reflects better measurement, and the surveys likely understate inequality. Poverty lines in 2021 PPP dollars.
```
