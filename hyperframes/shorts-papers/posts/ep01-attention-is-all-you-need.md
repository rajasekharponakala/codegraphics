## Description

```
Most AI language models of today use one architecture from a 2017 paper: the Transformer.

In this Short:
• Before 2017, recurrent networks read one word after the other. They were slow to train on long sentences.
• The Transformer uses only attention. It reads all the words at the same time.
• Attention connects each word to the other words. In the paper's Figure 4, two attention heads connect "its" to "Law".
• Each word has a query, a key and a value. The query and the keys give the weights for the values.
• Result: 28.4 BLEU on WMT 2014 English to German, more than 2 BLEU better than the best previous results. The training time was 3.5 days on 8 P100 GPUs.

Paper:
Vaswani, Shazeer, Parmar, Uszkoreit, Jones, Gomez, Kaiser, Polosukhin. "Attention Is All You Need". NeurIPS 2017.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762
```
