## Description

```
Tony Joseph's book Early Indians tells the story of our ancestors using ancient DNA. This free interactive atlas lets you explore it on a map.

• ~65,000 years ago: the First Indians arrive from Africa. They still make up 50–65% of our ancestry.
• After 7000 BCE: farmers from Iran's Zagros hills move east, mix with the First Indians and help build the Harappan civilization.
• 2600–1900 BCE: 700 years of Harappan cities with no sign of being attacked or burnt.
• After 2000 BCE: Steppe pastoralists arrive, bringing Indo-European languages.
• The "Indian pizza": a First Indian base, Zagros sauce, Steppe cheese and east Asian toppings.
```
