Sugar built capitalism

The first global commodity wasn't oil. It was sugar.

1226

England's King Henry III was ready to pay roughly US$450 in today's money for three pounds of sugar.

Sugar + people

Sugar travelled with a second 'commodity': enslaved Africans, sold in the Caribbean and the Americas.

Field + factory

Plantations grew, crushed and boiled the cane on one site, using enslaved labour at every step.

4–7×

Investments in the sugar colonies were four to seven times more profitable than investments in England.

1 in 5

By 1774, raw sugar made up one-fifth of all British imports, more than any other good.
