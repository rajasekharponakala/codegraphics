## Description

```
Food is personal. Nourishment is political. Dr Sylvia Karpagam's book The Meat of the Matter (HarperCollins India, 2026) traces how nutrients, identity, markets and policy shape what reaches our plates.

• Signals: 67% of children aged 6–59 months had some anaemia (NFHS-5); 48% of under-fives in the poorest households were underweight vs 19% in the richest (CNNS).
• Access: affordability, availability, acceptance, the plate, and health.
• Acceptance: 29% of women and 17% of men reported never eating fish, meat or eggs (NFHS-5).
• Nutrients: in one study of Indian children, protein digestibility was 87.4% for egg and 65.2% for mung bean.
• Myths: "India is essentially vegetarian", "anaemia is just iron", "restrictions are nutrition-neutral".
• School meals: budget, supplier, menu, served, eaten, outcome.
• Trade-offs: nutrition, climate, livelihood and rights, together.
```
