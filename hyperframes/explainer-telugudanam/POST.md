## Description

```
Telugudanam (తెలుగుదనం) means the essence of being Telugu. This free online museum tells the story of the language in five interactive halls:

• Script: every Telugu letter on one grid, native and borrowed sounds in different colours.
• Language: Telugu's Dravidian roots, and its closest relative, Tulu.
• Literature: from Burra Katha and Oggu Katha to Vemana's verses.
• Diaspora: from old sea trade to the Gulf and America (about 1,500 Telugus in the US in 1970, 1.2 million+ by 2024).
• Digital Telugu: from broken fonts to the Telugu Wikipedia (2003) and Unicode (2004).

This video only shows a glimpse. Visit the site and try every exhibit yourself, in English or Telugu:
https://ranjithraj.gitlab.io/telugudanam/

Footage: screen recordings of the site. Voice: AI text-to-speech. Music: original.
```
