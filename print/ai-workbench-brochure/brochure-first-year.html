Engineering is changing.AI tools now help write, explain and test code. The engineers who do well will be the ones who understand these tools, not just use them. Start in your first year, with tools you own and can see inside.

In this workshop you set up an AI assistant for your coursework and projects, step by step. It works with your own files, explains code in plain words, helps you test your programs and keeps your notes in order. You do not need any experience: if you have written a "Hello, world!", you are ready.

Step-by-step labsFrom zero to your own AI workbench

1
Set up your laptopInstall OpenCode and Git, and create your first project folder for a lab assignment.
2
Your first AI sessionAsk the assistant to explain a program line by line, then change it and see what happens.
3
Connect your filesUse MCP to let the assistant read your project folder, and decide exactly what it can access.
4
Use ready-made skillsSkills that explain code, write simple tests for your programs and turn lab work into notes.
5
See code as a mapDraw how the files and functions in a project connect, so a bigger program makes sense.
6
Make your own skillWrite one small skill or automation of your own, for example a checklist that reviews your lab code before you submit it.
