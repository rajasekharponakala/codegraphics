Engineering is changing.AI agents now read code, run tools and draft documentation. Most of them live on closed platforms. In this workshop you build an open one that you own, understand and can take anywhere.

You will configure an AI-assisted engineering harness that understands your project's context, works only with the tools and data sources you approve, and supports real engineering work, without depending on a proprietary platform. Guided labs take you from installation to your own custom skills and integrations.
